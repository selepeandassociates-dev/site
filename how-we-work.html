Our approach

Smarter workflows. Measurable performance.

Technology-enabled delivery is not a feature of the firm. It is how the firm operates. Every engagement is built around data, workflow discipline and reporting you can see without having to ask.

How we engage

Five steps from first meeting to measurable results

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We meet to understand your objectives, portfolio and challenges. Where relevant, we offer a complimentary review of a sample of your current portfolio to establish a factual baseline.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    We recommend the service model that fits, with transparent commercial terms.

  3. 03

    Onboarding

    Conflict checks, client verification, mandate and a service level agreement that records the firm’s professional independence, and configuration of your reporting.

  4. 04

    Delivery & reporting

    A dedicated team delivers against agreed targets, with structured reporting and regular management meetings.

  5. 05

    Review & scale

    We review results against targets and agree how to extend, adjust or expand the relationship.

Clients who prefer to test our approach first can begin with a proof of concept on a defined portion of their portfolio, or with a single subscription plan. There is no obligation to extend the relationship after a pilot. Where we resource a dedicated operating unit, the agreement carries an agreed term so that the team can be properly resourced.

Your partnership journey

Each step is your decision, based on results

We expect most institutional relationships to deepen over time. None of the steps is required.

  1. Step 1

    Legal Service Provider

    We act on individual matters or a subscription plan, and you see how we work.

  2. Step 2

    Panel Provider

    We are appointed to your panel for a defined category of work.

  3. Step 3

    Dedicated Team

    A ring-fenced team is assigned to your portfolio.

  4. Step 4

    Managed Function

    We run a defined function, such as recoveries or litigation administration.

  5. Step 5

    Managed Department

    We manage a full department on your behalf, within agreed governance arrangements.

  6. Step 6

    External Operating Partner

    We operate as an integrated partner across legal, recovery and operational functions, while remaining independent in our professional judgment.

Technology & innovation

Legal Interact

The firm’s core operating platform. It provides end-to-end management of matters from intake to finalisation, with structured workflows, document storage, performance monitoring and reporting.

Innovation in practice

  • Automation of routine tasks, so that attorney time is focused on judgement, negotiation and advocacy
  • Call recording that is secure and auditable, for training, compliance and reference
  • Dashboards and analytics that give clients visibility of recoveries, turnaround times, costs and portfolio ageing
  • Billing structured to be audit-ready and compatible with client e-billing and procurement systems
Supported areaHow the platform is used
RecoveriesAllocation, contact strategies, payment tracking and campaign management across large portfolios.
Liability claimsTracking of claim status, actions taken, communications and settlement progress.
CollectionsArrangement monitoring, call and payment statistics and ageing analysis.
LitigationTracking of summons, deadlines, court dates and expected trial matters, so that nothing is missed.
ReportingStructured weekly and monthly reports on matters initiated, finalised and pending, plus performance against targets.
Workflow managementTask allocation, escalation and audit trails that support consistent quality and accountability.
