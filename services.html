Advise, structure & protect

Foundations that protect growth

Labour & Employment Law

Employment is a business’s largest relationship and one of its largest sources of legal risk. We help employers keep it fair, compliant and defensible, from the first employment contract to the final disciplinary or CCMA hearing.

What we do

  • CCMA and Bargaining Council representation
  • Executive and general employment contract drafting
  • Workplace dispute resolution
  • Restructuring strategies
  • Disciplinary inquiries
  • Corporate labour compliance auditing

Corporate & Commercial Law

Strong commercial foundations protect growth. We structure, document and negotiate the agreements and corporate arrangements on which businesses depend.

What we do

  • Commercial agreement drafting, vetting and negotiation
  • Company registrations
  • Corporate governance frameworks
  • Transaction advisory services
  • Regulatory compliance monitoring
  • Joint venture set-up

Property, Conveyancing & Notarial Services

Property transactions are among the largest a client will make. We provide accurate, timely transfer and registration, together with the contracts and notarial formalities around them.

What we do

  • Deed registrations and property transfers
  • Real estate law
  • Drafting of commercial and residential lease and sale agreements
  • Community scheme compliance and sectional title disputes
  • Specialised notarial services

Family Law

Family matters call for discretion and sound judgment. We guide clients through separation and the arrangements for children and finances, favouring constructive resolution wherever it is possible.

What we do

  • Contested and uncontested divorces
  • Maintenance applications and adjustments
  • Parental rights, care and contact frameworks
  • Drafting of antenuptial contracts
  • Domestic dispute mediation

Estates, Trusts & Succession Planning

Protecting what a family or business has built, and passing it on as intended. We administer estates professionally and structure wills and trusts for lasting protection.

What we do

  • Winding up and professional administration of deceased estates
  • Drafting of wills and codicils
  • Registration and management of family, business and testamentary trusts
  • Long-term estate and succession restructuring

Delivered by qualified practitioners

Conveyancing, notarial and certain other services are performed by practitioners who hold the relevant admissions, in accordance with the Legal Practice Act and the applicable registry and court requirements.

Resolve, defend & recover

When a matter needs to be won, defended or recovered

Litigation & Dispute Resolution

When a dispute cannot be settled by negotiation, we pursue the route that best serves the client’s objective and budget, whether that is the courtroom, mediation or arbitration. Litigation remains a standalone service line, with billing aligned to the statutory tariff frameworks and stage-based budgets so you can reserve accurately.

  • High Court and Magistrates’ Court civil litigation
  • Corporate and commercial dispute resolution
  • Alternative dispute resolution, including mediation and arbitration
  • Urgent motion proceedings
  • Labour, insurance, liability and debt collection litigation; appeals and reviews

Insurance, Liability & Personal Injury

This is the firm’s home ground. We manage liability and insurance claims for insurers, corporates and individuals: protecting reserves, recovering losses and optimising outcomes for injured parties.

  • Third-party liability claims management
  • Insurance recovery actions and subrogation handling
  • Coverage dispute resolution
  • Risk assessment
  • Personal injury damages optimisation

Criminal Defence

A criminal charge can arrive without warning. We provide urgent, round-the-clock representation from the police station through trial and, where necessary, appeal.

  • 24/7 urgent bail applications (police station and court bail)
  • Trial representation in the Magistrates’, Regional and High Courts
  • Criminal litigation management
  • Formal statutory appeals

Debt Recovery & Credit Enforcement

Cash flow is the lifeblood of any organisation. We recover what is owed through performance-based collections, structured follow-up and, where necessary, litigation and enforcement.

  • Performance-based corporate and institutional collections
  • Credit enforcement litigation
  • Automated and soft collections workflows
  • Warrant of execution management
  • Systemic asset recovery
