How subscriptions work

Clear scope, a defined monthly capacity and a written agreement, so you always know what you are getting.

  • {{icon:clock:h-7 w-7}}

    Monthly capacity

    Capacity is allocated to each subscriber every month and does not roll over to the following month.

  • {{icon:gavel:h-7 w-7}}

    Litigation is separate

    Matters that move into formal proceedings, or that need counsel or significant disbursements, are scoped separately at preferential subscriber rates.

  • {{icon:scale-up:h-7 w-7}}

    Grow when you need to

    Annual payment options are available at a discount, and you may upgrade as your needs grow.

  • {{icon:doc:h-7 w-7}}

    Written agreement

    Every plan is governed by a written subscription agreement. Business plan fees are quoted exclusive of VAT.
