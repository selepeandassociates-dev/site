Founder & leadership

Thabo Isaac Selepe

Founder, Managing Director & Executive Director / CEO

LLB, Cert. Compliance Management (UP), Cert. Management Principles (UNISA), FAIS Accredited

Thabo Selepe is a legal professional with more than seventeen years of experience in insurance legal services, pre-litigation, recoveries, liability management and litigation, complemented by formal qualifications in compliance management and management principles, and FAIS accreditation.

His career has been built in the operational heart of the insurance legal environment, where he has led pre-litigation and recoveries operations serving insurers and corporate clients. That experience, which combines legal practice with portfolio management, reporting and commercial discipline, shaped the integrated service model on which Selepe & Associates Inc is built.

He is committed to building a firm that earns client trust through transparency, measurable results and consistent standards, and to developing the people and systems that allow those standards to scale.

Governance, compliance & ethics

How we handle your money, information and matters

{{icon:scales:h-8 w-8}}

Professional independence

We are an independent law firm. Our professional judgment, advice and conduct of matters are not subject to direction by any client or commercial arrangement, and our duties to the court and the law come first.

{{icon:doc:h-8 w-8}}

Professional regulation

We practise in accordance with the Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014, the rules of the Legal Practice Council and the Code of Conduct.

{{icon:lock:h-8 w-8}}

Client funds

Money held on behalf of clients is dealt with in line with the trust accounting requirements of the Legal Practice Act, with business and trust accounts kept strictly separate.

{{icon:filepen:h-8 w-8}}

Fee agreements

Contingency arrangements are documented in writing and structured in line with the Contingency Fees Act.

{{icon:check:h-8 w-8}}

Client verification & conflicts

FICA verification and conflict checking precede every engagement.

{{icon:shield:h-8 w-8}}

Data protection

Client and third-party personal information is handled in line with POPIA.

{{icon:handshake:h-8 w-8}}

Fair treatment

Our work in the insurance environment is guided by the principles of Treating Customers Fairly.

{{icon:coins:h-8 w-8}}

Debt collection

Collections work is carried out in accordance with the applicable debt-collector regulatory requirements.

{{icon:chart:h-8 w-8}}

Audit & quality

Call recording, file audits and structured reporting support continuous improvement and accountability.
