Independence is built into every arrangement

Client oversight applies to service levels, cost, reporting and strategy. It does not extend to directing our professional opinions, or the conduct of a matter in a way that would compromise our duties to the court, the law or our professional obligations.

Commercial model

A multi-year managed services agreement structured around agreed service levels, reporting and performance measures. Clients who prefer to begin smaller can start with a proof of concept on a defined portion of their portfolio.

Our independence safeguards

  • Legal advice is given on its merits. It is not shaped by a client’s preferred outcome or by our fee arrangement.
  • We decline instructions that conflict with our duties to the court, the law or our professional and ethical obligations.
  • Performance targets measure service, such as turnaround, recoveries and cost. They are never conditions on the content of our advice.
  • Confidentiality and conflict checks are maintained for every client, and no client has access to another client’s information or files.
  • Where our people work closely with a client’s teams, they remain under our supervision, and we remain accountable for their professional conduct.
  • Trust accounting, fee arrangements and file management remain under the firm’s control.

Who it suits: insurers, banks, universities, municipalities, fleet operators and large corporates that want the consistency, institutional knowledge and management reporting of a dedicated function without the fixed cost of building one.

How a recovery matter is managed

Every recovery matter moves through four stages, and our fees are structured to follow results through each of them.

  1. 01

    Recovery success

    Securing the recovery or settlement itself, which is the primary measure of performance.

  2. 02

    Legal cost recovery

    Recovering the legal costs of pursuing the matter from the responsible party where the law and the agreement allow.

  3. 03

    Taxed cost recovery

    Preparing bills of costs and pursuing taxation so that costs awarded are properly quantified and recovered.

  4. 04

    Enforcement recovery

    Enforcing judgments and settlements so that money owed is actually collected.

How fees apply at each stage is set out in the written agreement for the matter, and is always structured in line with the Contingency Fees Act and the rules of the Legal Practice Council.

Our commercial approach

Fee structures that follow results

Contingency, success-based, recovery-based, cost recovery and hybrid pricing models, agreed in writing before work begins.

  • No retainer

    On recovery and pre-litigation work. Our fee is earned when a recovery is achieved.

  • Volume-based fees

    Our contingency rate reduces as the volume of claims allocated to us increases.

  • Litigation by consensus

    A retainer becomes payable only where there is mutual agreement that litigation is the right next step, with costs billed progressively.

  • Tariff-aligned litigation

    Fees are bound to the statutory tariff frameworks, and we pursue cost orders for the client.

  • Disbursements paid upfront

    Sheriff fees, tracing, counsel and expert fees are paid upfront into our trust account, applied only to the matter, passed through at cost without mark-up, and fully accounted for.
