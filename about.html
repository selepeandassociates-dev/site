About the firm

A law firm that behaves like a dependable partner.

Selepe & Associates Inc brings legal practice, governance support, recovery expertise and operational capacity together under one accountable structure, while remaining independent in the advice it gives.

Who we are

We act for individuals, families, professionals, growing businesses, corporates and institutions, including insurers, financial institutions, municipalities, universities and property schemes.

Our objective is to move beyond transactional legal engagements and become a trusted, long-term strategic partner to our clients.

Legal Solutions

Advice, drafting, litigation and representation across nine areas of legal service.

Business Support

Subscription legal support, corporate legal services, managed legal operations and external operating partner arrangements that give an organisation legal capacity without the overhead.

Recovery Expertise

Performance-based recovery and liability management, supported by a national correspondent network, call-centre operations and a purpose-built case management platform.

A message from our founder

People buy into leaders. Here is plainly why this firm exists.

I established Selepe & Associates Inc because I believe legal services can be delivered differently: more practically, more commercially, and with far greater accountability than many clients have come to expect.

Traditional firms sell legal advice, litigation, professional time and legal opinions. Over more than seventeen years in the insurance legal environment, working across pre-litigation, recoveries, liability management and litigation, I have seen what organisations actually need from their legal partners: problems resolved, money recovered, risk reduced, and someone who takes ownership. Too often the work is fragmented across providers, priced in ways clients cannot forecast, and measured by activity rather than outcomes.

Why the firm was established

A law firm should be a dependable, closely integrated partner to its clients, while remaining independent in the advice it gives. That means combining legal authority with operational discipline, so that one accountable team can advise, negotiate, recover and, where necessary, litigate. Where we recover on a client’s behalf, we are rewarded when the client is rewarded, but never at the expense of our professional judgment.

Our commitment to clients

  • A single point of accountability for every matter, from first advice to final outcome
  • Direct access to qualified legal professionals who understand your business, not just your file
  • Transparent pricing, with fee structures that align our interests with yours
  • Honest, timely communication, including when the news is difficult
  • Independent professional advice, given on its merits even when it is not what you hoped to hear

The long-term vision

The ambition is a firm of consequence: multi-disciplinary, nationally capable and trusted across sectors, serving individuals and institutions with the same standard of excellence. I invite you to test our approach, hold us to measurable targets, and judge us by what we deliver.

Yours in service, Thabo Isaac Selepe

Independent by principle

We work closely with our clients and integrate with their processes, but our professional judgment, the advice we give and the way we conduct matters remain our own. No client or commercial arrangement is permitted to direct our professional opinions, compromise our duties to the court, or override our ethical obligations.
